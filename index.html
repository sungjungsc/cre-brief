Capital markets & macro

The Fed held at 3.75–4.00% on Oct 28 on a 10–2 vote, with markets pricing a possible hike at the Dec 8–9 meeting (single-source, snippet est.). No new 10-year Treasury reading surfaced this week; the last confirmed level was about 5.10% in mid-October, after a 5.365% peak on Oct 7. The Freddie Mac 30-year mortgage rate eased to 7.18% for the week of Nov 5, from 7.24% the week before and 7.40% in early/mid-October. On the multifamily side, the latest available RealPage data (Q2 2026) shows average cap rates of 5.79%, the highest quarterly average since Q3 2015, on roughly $36.7B of volume that is flat year over year but about a third below the five-year average. Walker & Dunlop likewise reports dollar volume near pre-COVID levels with deal count about 20% lower. Sources: Admiral Markets 10-28; FRED/Freddie Mac 11-05; RealPage Q2'26; W&D Q2'26
