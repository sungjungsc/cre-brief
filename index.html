Capital markets & macro

Watch items

  1. Rate path: 10-yr at 5.28% (highest since 2002) with 16 of 18 Fed participants projecting another hike; Oct 27–28 FOMC is the next data point for debt costs versus ~5.2% multifamily cap rates.
  2. Sunbelt supply taper: Austin (~10.2k vs 30k), San Antonio (~2.6k, −63%) and Dallas (12 straight quarters of pipeline contraction) show deliveries falling; Charlotte concessions (65% of listings) still elevated. Rents still negative or flat YoY on most indices.
  3. National rents: Apartment List's first monthly dip since January (−0.1%) while YoY improved to −0.4% from −1.6% in April; Apartments.com shows +1.5% YoY — index divergence persists.
  4. Student housing: preleasing at records (Yardi 93%, RealPage 91%) but leasing-season rent growth slowed to 1.1%; UF still has Fall 2026 availability and GSU is waitlisted on campus.
  5. Hotels: September RevPAR running double-digit weekly gains (+14.2%); OKC and Burbank figures remain unpublished in accessible sources.
