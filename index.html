Capital markets & macro

The 10-year Treasury climbed to a three-year-high area before easing back to 4.77% by Thursday's close (+10 bps on the week) as dovish remarks from Fed Governor Waller pulled yields down late in the week. Even so, Waller's own September 3 speech — warning inflation remains "meaningfully above" the Fed's 2% goal — reinforced market pricing of roughly two-thirds odds for a 25-basis-point HIKE at the September 15–16 FOMC meeting, a continuation of the hawkish pivot flagged last week. The Fed funds rate remains held at 3.50–3.75% since its July 29 decision. Cap rates continue to stabilize: Class A multifamily in primary markets trades at 4.50%–5.25%, with most property types expected to compress a further 5–15 bps in 2026 as the post-2023 repricing cycle finds its new equilibrium.
