Capital markets & macro

The 10-year Treasury was last confirmed near 5.28% (10-02 close, carried forward; no newer close surfaced), after touching levels not seen since 2002–2007 in late September. The Fed raised its target range 25 bp to 3.75–4.00% on 09-16, with the next FOMC on Oct 27–28 and futures odds of another October move last seen in the mid-teens percent. Freddie Mac's 30-year mortgage rate held at 7.40% for the week of 10-15 (7.28% on 10-01). Transaction cap rates sat around 6.3% in Q2 with the spread over the 10-year near historic lows; CBRE's H1 survey showed cap rates roughly unchanged but a growing share of respondents expecting increases. Cap-rate figures are more than 30 days old. Sources: FRED/Freddie Mac 10-15, TradingEconomics 10-02, First American, CBRE H1
