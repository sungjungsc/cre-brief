Capital markets & macro

The 10-year Treasury eased to 4.88% on 11-09 (4.84–4.86% on 11-05/06), down about 22 bps from 5.10% in mid-October and off early-October levels above 5.25%. Policy risk points the other way: October payrolls came in at +165k (nearly 3× consensus) with unemployment at 4.1%. Futures now price roughly a 77–80% chance of a 25 bp hike at the Dec 8–9 FOMC, up from ~67–72%. The Fed held at 3.75–4.00% on 10-28. Freddie Mac's 30-yr rate was 7.18% (wk of 11-05, carried). In apartment capital markets, IPA describes the Q3 sales recovery as slow and uneven, with the cap-rate spread over Treasuries squeezed for 17 straight quarters. NMHC's Q3 sales-volume index read 46 (below 50 = lower volume). Small-multifamily cap rates averaged 6.0% in Q2 (5.8% in Q1). Headline annual apartment sales of $208.1B are inflated by the AvalonBay–Equity Residential merger, and deal count is down. Sources: FRED/YCharts 11-09; BLS/CME via InvestmentNews 11-06; IPA, NMHC Q3'26; Arbor/Chandan Q2'26

Watch items

  1. Dec 8–9 FOMC: hike odds are ~77–80% after the strong October jobs report. The November jobs report (Dec 4) is the last major input. The 10-yr has eased to ~4.88% anyway, so the long end and the policy rate are moving in opposite directions.
  2. National rents turned positive YoY (+0.1%, Apartment List Oct data) for the first time since mid-2023. Watch whether Sun Belt held metros follow or lag through the winter off-season.
  3. Texas supply runoff: San Antonio's 2026 deliveries are down ~63% and Dallas absorption exceeds deliveries, but Austin and San Antonio concessions are still deep (Austin: 37% of units, ~15% average discount).
  4. Student housing: Yardi expects fall occupancy below 2025 and slower rent growth (+1.1%), with 18,500 beds under construction. Gainesville and Ann Arbor have the most visible new near-campus pipelines.
  5. Hotel momentum: national RevPAR is up ~10% YoY in late October against soft November 2025 comps, but market-level reads for Austin, LA/Burbank and OKC are missing.
