01

Capital markets & macro

The 10-year Treasury crossed 5.00% on September 14 — its highest level since October 2023 — with readings this week ranging 4.80%–5.00%. The FOMC followed on September 16 with a 25bp hike to 3.75%–4.00% (12-0 vote), the first rate increase since 2023, resolving last week's split-odds watch item. The Fed cited elevated inflation; updated projections show 16 of 18 participants expecting at least one more hike (four see two more), with markets currently pricing one additional 25bp move before year-end and further hikes seen extending into 2027. The move is already flowing through to CRE financing: Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed CRE mortgage rate reached 6.76% in mid-September (up from 6.67% a month earlier) — the highest CRE borrowing cost in over a year — as fixed-rate capital sources (agencies, life companies, CMBS conduits) reprice directly off the higher Treasury curve. That puts the 5–15bp cap-rate compression CBRE's H1 2026 survey had projected for the year in question. Sources: 09-14, 09-16, 09-18
