01

Capital markets & macro

The 10-year Treasury closed at 5.051% on 11-19, its fourth straight down day, but still about 17 bps above the 4.88% in last week's brief. It was 4.98% on 11-10 and roughly 5.15% on 11-13 (snippet est.); the 52-week high is 5.31% (10-05). The move came from October CPI: headline +0.4% MoM against ~0.3% expected, and core +0.3% for a third straight month. BLS published no YoY figure because of the Oct-2025 shutdown gap. Fed Governor Waller said a 50 bp hike should be considered. CME FedWatch now prices a December hike to 4.00–4.25% as near-certain, with about 14% odds of 50 bps. The funds rate is 3.75–4.00% after the 10-28 hold, where two members dissented in favor of a hike. On the CRE side, Q3 multifamily sales were $32.9B, +30.8% QoQ and −10.5% YoY (NAA), and small-multifamily lending is running at a $79.1B annualized pace, up 6% (Arbor/CRE Daily). Cap rates are still near 6% (small MF, Q2). CMBS cap rates sit roughly level with loan coupons, so leverage is thin; agency debt averages 4.98% vs 6.44% for conduit. RealPage national occupancy held at 95.4% in Q3. Sources: DJ/Morningstar 11-19; InvestmentNews/NMN/Continuum mid-Nov; NAA Q3; CRE Daily Q3; CRED iQ as of 06-16

05

Watch items

  1. Dec 8–9 FOMC. A hike to 4.00–4.25% is near-fully priced, and 50 bps is on the table. Nov jobs (12-04) is the last big data point before the meeting. With the 10-yr around 5%, refi and exit-cap assumptions get tested again.
  2. Fall 2027 student pre-lease stalled at 3.8%. October usually adds 5–6 pts and didn't. Watch whether November and December catch up, especially at UF and Michigan, where large new bed counts are coming.
  3. Sunbelt supply is turning. Austin deliveries are down ~74%, DFW absorption is set to triple by Q3'27, and San Antonio may face a shortage by late 2027. Even so, all four oversupply metros still post negative YoY rents.
  4. Thin leverage. CMBS cap rates are roughly level with coupons. Agency debt (~4.98%) is the cheapest source as conduit pricing (6.44%) tracks Treasuries higher.
  5. Apartment List November-data report is due early December. It should show whether the first positive national YoY print (+0.1%) holds through the off-season.
