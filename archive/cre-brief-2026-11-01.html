Capital markets & macro

The Fed held the funds rate at 3.75–4.00% on October 28 in a 10–2 vote, and markets are pricing a hike at the December 8–9 meeting. The 10-year Treasury was 5.10% on October 15 (latest confirmed print; it peaked at 5.365% on October 7, a 24-year high, and was ~5.12% around October 21). No later close surfaced. Multifamily cap rates have been reported near 5.7% (held seven quarters, stale); a CRE Daily headline cites CBRE saying cap rates could rise 40–60 bps with the 10-year above 5%, and CBRE's H1 survey found buyers waiting for the 10-year to fall toward ~4.6%. Average CRE loan rates are cited near 6.2% against ~4.8% on maturing debt. Sources: Admiral Markets 10-28, FRED 10-15, CRE Daily, CBRE H1 survey (Aug), Lev
