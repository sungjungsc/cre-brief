Capital markets & macro

The 10-year Treasury held roughly flat at ~4.23% this week, steady since the sharp ~40bp drop two weeks ago. The Fed held its benchmark rate at 3.50–3.75% at the July 29 FOMC meeting on a 9-3 vote — three dissents in favor of a hike, the first unified hawkish dissent bloc since 2016 — with the next meeting confirmed for September 16; market-implied odds of a September hike have eased to ~60% as headline inflation cooled to 3.5% even as core inflation (2.6%) stays above target. 05

Watch items

  1. September 16 FOMC is the next rate catalyst — market-implied hike odds have already eased to ~60% from ~79% pre-July-meeting; watch the next two CPI prints.
  2. Sunbelt supply cycle turning: Austin and San Antonio deliveries are falling sharply into 2026 (−63% YoY in San Antonio), which should ease rent pressure into 2027 — but Dallas and Charlotte pipelines are still winding down and bear watching for absorption.
  3. Multi-source rent YoY divergence (Dallas, Houston, Indianapolis, Columbus) remains wide and unreconciled across aggregators — treat any single-source read with caution until Apartment List's own metro figures resurface.
  4. Student housing campuses (GSU, UF, Michigan, Alabama, USC-Columbia) are now 2-3 weeks stale on search coverage; refresh priority next cycle.
  5. LA hospitality is the only major US market with negative occupancy YoY, tied to the phased $30/hr hotel/airport minimum wage — worth tracking alongside World Cup-related demand tailwinds.
